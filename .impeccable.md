## Design Context

### Users
- **Primary audience:** Young Iraqi freelancers and clients, 18-35, tech-savvy
- **Context of use:** Mobile-first (Android dominant market), used on-the-go and at desk. Users browse tasks, negotiate prices, chat with providers, and manage payments — often with intermittent connectivity.
- **Job to be done:** Clients find and hire skilled freelancers quickly. Freelancers discover work, bid competitively, deliver results, and get paid securely.
- **Emotional goals:** The interface should feel **modern** (this is how things should work), **trustworthy** (my money and work are safe), and **professional** (I'm using a serious platform, not a toy).
- **Cultural context:** Arabic language support via Noto Sans Arabic fallback. Right-to-left readiness for future localization. Iraqi dinar (IQD) and QiCard payment integration. Users expect app quality comparable to international platforms.

### Brand Personality
- **Three words:** Friendly, Approachable, Smart
- **Voice:** Warm but competent. Like a knowledgeable friend who helps you get things done — not a corporate robot, not a casual buddy.
- **Tone spectrum:** Encouraging when onboarding, clear when transacting, calm when errors occur, celebratory when milestones happen.
- **Brand colors:**
  - Teal (#146068) — primary: trust, professionalism, calm confidence
  - Gold (#f3bb19) — accent: achievement, premium, attention
  - LightTeal (#93ccce) — secondary: approachability, freshness
  - LightRed (#f49d9d) — danger only: errors, destructive actions, urgency

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone:** Clean, contemporary, solid surfaces with clear depth hierarchy. NO glassmorphism, NO frosted glass, NO BackdropFilter blur effects anywhere in the app.
- **Mood:** Bright and airy in light mode, deep and focused in dark mode (dark mode is default).
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans — geometric, friendly, professional. Bold weights for hierarchy, regular for reading comfort.
- **Spacing:** Generous whitespace. Content breathes. Cards don't feel cramped.
- **Motion:** Purposeful, not decorative. Ease-out curves for natural deceleration. Micro-interactions on taps (scale, haptics). Staggered list entrances. No bounce, no elastic, no gratuitous animation.
- **Depth:** Achieved through elevation shadows (4-level scale) and subtle borders — NOT through blur or glass effects. Layered content with clear z-index hierarchy.
- **Surfaces:** Solid color backgrounds from `colorScheme.cardBackground`. Subtle borders from `colorScheme.cardBorder`. Elevation shadows from `AppDecorations.elevation()`. No transparency gimmicks.
- **Iconography:** Material Icons (already in use). Outlined style for inactive, filled for active. Consistent sizing.

- **References (aspirational feel):**
  - Wise (fintech) — clean, trustworthy, great empty states, solid surfaces
  - Linear (project management) — fast, keyboard-first, excellent dark mode, no glass
  - Revolut (banking) — premium card design, fluid animations, clear hierarchy

- **Anti-references (explicitly avoid):**
  - Old Fiverr / Khamsat — cluttered, overwhelming, cheap marketplace feel
  - Generic Material / Flutter templates — stock look, default widgets, cookie-cutter
  - Overly playful / cartoon apps — excessive emojis, gamification, childish elements
  - AI slop aesthetics — cyan gradients, purple-to-blue, gradient text, hero metric grids, identical card arrays
  - **Glassmorphism / frosted glass** — BackdropFilter blur effects, semi-transparent glass cards, frosted overlays. These are performance-heavy, look dated in 2026, and add visual noise without improving usability.

### Design Principles

1. **Content First, Chrome Second**
   Every pixel of decoration must earn its place. If removing an element doesn't reduce understanding, remove it. Users come for tasks, bids, and chat — not for visual effects.

2. **Clarity Over Cleverness**
   When in doubt, be obvious. Labels should be scannable. Actions should be unmistakable. Error messages should tell you what happened and what to do. No jargon, no mystery meat navigation.

3. **Rewarding Interactions**
   Every tap should feel acknowledged. Buttons scale, badges animate, lists stagger in, success states flash. Haptic feedback on all primary actions. The app should feel alive, not dead.

4. **Consistent and Predictable**
   Same patterns everywhere. Cards look like cards. Buttons look like buttons. Navigation behaves the same on every screen. Design tokens (colors, spacing, typography, motion) are the single source of truth — zero hardcoded values in feature code.

5. **Accessible by Default**
   WCAG AA minimum. 4.5:1 contrast ratios. 48px touch targets. Semantic labels on all interactive elements. Focus indicators for keyboard/switch access. Reduced motion support. Color is never the only indicator — always pair with icon or text.

### Theme Configuration
- **Default mode:** Dark
- **Light mode:** Supported, full parity
- **Font family:** Plus Jakarta Sans (Google Fonts) with Noto Sans, Noto Sans Arabic, Roboto, Arial, sans-serif fallbacks
- **Base grid:** 4px
- **Screen margins:** 24px horizontal
- **Border radius scale:** r4, r8, r10, r12, r14, r16, r18, r20, r23, r24, r30, r50
- **Elevation levels:** 0 (flat), 1 (resting), 2 (raised), 3 (floating), 4 (dramatic)
- **Animation durations:** 100ms (micro), 200ms (fast), 300ms (standard), 500ms (emphasis), 800ms (dramatic)
- **Animation curves:** ease-out family (easeOutCubic standard, easeOutQuart decel, easeOutExpo sharp). Never bounce or elastic.
- **Surface style:** Solid `cardBackground` color + `cardBorder` border + `AppDecorations.elevation()` shadow. NO glass, NO blur, NO BackdropFilter.

### NO GLASS POLICY (Mandatory for All Agents)

**This is a hard rule. No exceptions. No "just this one element."**

- **NEVER** use `BackdropFilter` for visual blur effects.
- **NEVER** use `GlassContainer`, `GlassCard`, or `GlassButton` in new code. These widgets exist in the codebase from legacy design and will be migrated to solid-surface equivalents in Phase 1.
- **NEVER** use semi-transparent gradient backgrounds as a glass substitute.
- **Bottom navigation bar:** Replace frosted glass with solid surface (cardBackground + elevation shadow + border).
- **Modals and sheets:** Solid cardBackground, not frosted overlays.
- **Cards:** Solid cardBackground + subtle cardBorder + elevation shadow.
- **Reason:** Glass effects are performance-heavy (BackdropFilter forces rasterization), look dated in 2026, add visual complexity without improving usability, and hurt readability on busy backgrounds.
- **What to use instead:** Solid colors from `colorScheme`, borders from `colorScheme.cardBorder`, depth from `AppDecorations.elevation()`. These are faster, cleaner, and more accessible.
